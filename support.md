## Common questions

**Why isn't my photo cropped?**
That's the point of Matte: the whole photo is fitted inside the canvas and the leftover space becomes the border, so nothing is cut off.

**Where do saved photos go?**
On iPhone and iPad, **Save to Photos** adds them to your Photos library as new photos. Your originals stay untouched.

**Where do Mac exports go?**
To the folder you choose when you click **Export…**. Matte remembers that folder and opens there next time.

**Does Matte upload my photos?**
No. Everything happens on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**How do I remove a photo from the batch?**
Tap the ✕ on the selected thumbnail, press and hold a thumbnail and choose **Remove**, or tap **Select** to remove several at once. You can undo right after.

**Can I keep the location in my photos?**
Yes. Location is removed by default; turn off **Remove Location** (in the Export sheet on iPhone and iPad, or the sidebar on Mac) to keep it.
